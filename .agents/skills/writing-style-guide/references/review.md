# Editorial and copy review

Read the passage for its meaning before looking for patterns. Scale the review to the requested scope and consider
the rules for its actual placement. A concise edit that removes a necessary condition is a worse edit.

## Meaning, structure and voice

- Check whether the main point, decision or action is buried, context is missing, references are ambiguous, or steps
  depend on information introduced too late. Give each passage a useful job.
- Let purpose determine structure. An argument needs evidence and reasoning; instructions need usable steps;
  a narrative may need pacing or suspense. Technical explanations do not need a sales pitch in every sentence.
- Check register, emphasis and humor against the target voice. A requested brand transformation should change how
  the draft sounds while preserving meaning and factual uncertainty.
- Read neighboring sentences together. Look for a sequence of identical setups, fragments or punchlines. Retain
  consistent structures when the guide prescribes them for comparable headings, instructions, lists or tables.

## Evidence and specificity

Distinguish objective claims from opinions, values, intentions and hypothetical possibilities. Check that an edit
preserves those distinctions and the supported scope. A memorable line must not quietly change what is promised.

Use available actors, actions, mechanisms, examples, dates and amounts to replace vague praise. Numbers are useful
when supported, not compulsory. Identify a missing fact rather than inventing a metric, testimonial, quote, guarantee,
endorsement or personal experience. Flag suspected factual errors separately from wording suggestions.

Check attribution, causal links, statistics and their qualifications. Preserve precise specialist language and enough
context for the intended audience. Fluency is not evidence that the underlying claim is true.

## Formulaic writing

Read [deslopify-writing](../../deslopify-writing/SKILL.md) for the exact phrase list, broader checks and contextual
examples. Keep meaningful contrasts, accumulated hypothetical lists, genuine FAQs and humor that carries an insight.
Apply the detailed editorial prescriptions for the content's placement alongside the pattern review.

## Marketing and conversion copy

Use the following checks when the purpose calls for persuasion, with clarity and factual accuracy throughout:

- **Reader benefit:** connect important capabilities to consequences the audience cares about when supported.
- **Proof:** support consequential claims with available evidence; identify missing proof or narrow the claim.
- **Specificity:** show the relevant mechanism or example. Avoid invented precision.
- **Emotion:** use relevant worry, aspiration, relief or delight. Keep emotion grounded in the subject rather than
  manufacturing fear, intimacy or urgency.
- **Action and objections:** make the next step and its consequences understandable. Address relevant price,
  commitment, setup, cancellation and privacy questions with known facts. A trial, refund promise or risk reversal
  must be a real part of the offer.

For refreshes, check wording tied to old dates, product behavior, prices, links and evidence. Use available current
material or identify what needs verification.

## Deliver a requested rewrite

Return one finished version in the requested format, with a brief explanation of material changes when useful.
If the user requests clean copy, omit commentary. Don't expose internal drafts or add a formulaic closing paragraph.
