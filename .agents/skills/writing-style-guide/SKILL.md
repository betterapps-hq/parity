# Writing style guide

Use this guide to shape expression and apply concrete writing conventions. Preserve the message, facts,
qualifications and commitments while changing diction, tone, rhythm and structure to the requested voice.
For a point edit, stay within the requested passage and the context needed to make it work.

Review requests receive suggestions, not file changes. Edit files only when requested.

## Apply the guide

1. Establish audience, purpose, placement, target voice and the requested scope. Use available brand guidance,
   product facts and voice samples. Identify the decisions or actions the text must support.
2. Read the [core style prescriptions](references/voice-and-basics.md#top-10-style-and-voice-tips) for every writing
   task, then the relevant detailed entries. Preserve distinctions such as prose versus UI, general versus technical
   audiences, and translated versus locally written content. Keep the rule's exception with its prescription.
3. Draft or propose changes using those rules. State concrete actors, actions and consequences when known. Use
   supported examples and evidence; identify missing information rather than inventing precision.
4. Read [deslopify-writing](../deslopify-writing/SKILL.md) and apply its exact phrase checks and broader catalog.
   Explain the problem in context instead of replacing flagged words with equally vague synonyms.
5. Read the result as a whole. Recheck factual scope, conditions, certainty, attribution and commitments. Confirm that
   the result has the intended voice and still follows the conventions for its placement.

## Voice and expression

For ordinary product communication, use a voice that is warm and relaxed, crisp and clear, and ready to help. Adapt
tone to the reader's situation. Put the useful information where it can be found, use conversational wording and make
choices and next steps understandable.

For distinctive brand expression, bring these characteristics together across the piece:

- **Kind:** communicate warmth and respect for the reader's circumstances.
- **Daring:** use fresh, confident, energetic expression that avoids generic claims.
- **Deep:** consider other perspectives and connect the subject to an insight that matters to the audience.

The internal Trailblazer persona sees possibilities, acts with courage and brings others along. Use that perspective
to write; it is not a public label for the organization or an invented claim about the author.

| Mode | Intended effect | Useful placements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reveal | Inform, engage and give understanding or insight. | Explanations, instructions and body copy. |
| Inspire | Excite, delight and help the reader see a possibility. | Headlines and prominent passages. |
| Provoke | Challenge an assumption or express a position. | Campaign headlines, arguments and statements of values. |

Read [creative techniques](references/voice-techniques.md) for the selected mode before using a device. Longer pieces
can combine modes. Let interface controls and instructions communicate clearly; use the creative techniques where
they fit the placement. A requested voice transformation should change how the draft sounds. Preserve the original
style only when the user asks for it or the task is ordinary cleanup without a voice change.

## Anti-slop review

Read [deslopify-writing](../deslopify-writing/SKILL.md) before drafting, reviewing or delivering text with this guide.
It owns the exact pattern and phrase list, broader recurring constructions and contextual examples. Apply it alongside the
relevant style rules: intentional parallel headings, term lists, precise vocabulary and useful creative devices
serve different purposes from mechanical repetition and empty emphasis.

## Deliver the review or copy

For a review, lead with the most consequential issue. Show the exact passage, explain the reader-facing effect,
identify the applicable writing rule and propose a concrete replacement. Distinguish factual or meaning corrections,
deviations from this guide and optional creative directions. Preserve effective passages and group repeated findings.

For a requested rewrite, provide finished copy in the requested format. Include only the explanation or alternatives
the task calls for. Preserve quotations verbatim unless their modification is requested. Check dates, amounts,
conditions, evidence and promises against the brief before delivery. A style review does not verify an unsupported
claim or establish endorsement or legal approval.
